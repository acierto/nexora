# Refund policy

Refunds return money to the original payment method. Customer service and the
`refunds` API both use the rules below.

## Refund window

Orders can be refunded in full within 30 days of delivery. Between 30 and 90 days
only store credit is offered, unless the item was faulty. After 90 days refunds
need approval from the payments lead.

## Partial refunds

A partial refund never exceeds the captured amount minus earlier refunds. Shipping
costs are refunded only when the whole order is returned.

## Provider limits

The payment provider rejects more than 10 partial refunds per capture. Batch small
adjustments into one refund when you can.
